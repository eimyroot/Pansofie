# PANSOFIE MASTER ARCHITECTURE V1.0

Status: CANONICAL BASELINE
Effective: 2026-08-16

## Definition

**PANSOFIE is a system of lifelong development through real-world experiences that connects school, family and community so that a person can discover, act, document, reflect, transfer learning and contribute.**

Canonical loop:

`ČLOVĚK → MISE → SKUTEČNÁ ZKUŠENOST → DŮKAZ → REFLEXE → PORTFOLIO → SPOLUPRÁCE → DOPAD`

Apps, labs, AI models, events and business models are subordinate layers.

## 1. Seven development paths

1. **Já & wellbeing** — health, identity, self-reflection, resilience, self-management, safe habits.
2. **Poznání & myšlení** — learning-to-learn, critical thinking, information literacy, systems thinking, media/digital literacy.
3. **Vztahy & spolupráce** — empathy, communication, teamwork, conflict handling, intergenerational cooperation.
4. **Tvorba & řešení problémů** — creativity, inquiry, design, technology, craft, experimentation, innovation.
5. **Samostatnost & podnikavost** — planning, financial literacy, work, resources, projects, initiative, entrepreneurship.
6. **Občanství & přínos** — service, participation, democracy, community responsibility, public value.
7. **Příroda & udržitelnost** — nature, food systems, climate, biodiversity, circularity, regeneration, long-term systems impact.

Cross-cutting values: dignity, truthfulness, responsibility, respect, courage, fairness, care and service. Values are not a human ranking axis.

## 2. PANSOFIE Method

Internal method:

1. **OBJEV** — identify a real problem, question or need.
2. **PROZKOUMEJ** — gather facts, perspectives and context.
3. **NAVRHNI** — choose a plan, hypothesis or decision.
4. **UDĚLEJ** — perform real action.
5. **DOLOŽ** — record proportionate evidence of result.
6. **REFLEKTUJ** — identify what worked, failed and changed understanding.
7. **PŘENES** — state where learning can be used again.
8. **PŘISPĚJ** — identify value created for another person, group or environment.
9. **NAVAZUJ** — choose a meaningful next step.

Public UX remains simpler:

`OBJEV → UDĚLEJ → POCHOP → PŘISPĚJ`

## 3. Experience Standard — primary methodological IP

- **Mission** = assignment/opportunity.
- **Experience** = completed real-world learning event with evidence and reflection.

A canonical Experience defines:
- WHY
- MISSION
- CONTEXT
- PEOPLE
- PATHS
- ACTION
- EVIDENCE
- REFLECTION
- TRANSFER
- IMPACT
- NEXT
- SAFETY
- TEACHER_LOAD when used in school
- CURRICULUM_MAPPING when applicable

## 4. PANSOFIE Lens — systems-thinking tool

Three epistemic questions:

### FACTS
What do we know? What is evidence? What remains uncertain?

### EXPERIENCE
Who is affected? What do people actually experience?

### MEANING
What values, goals and long-term consequences are involved?

Then examine impact across five lenses:
1. Person
2. Society
3. Nature
4. Economy/resources
5. Technology

Do not collapse this into one “Harmony Score”. Use an **Impact Map** that exposes evidence, assumptions, uncertainty and trade-offs.

## 5. Experience Passport

The Passport is a longitudinal evidence portfolio, not a popularity profile.

A record may include:
- experience ID,
- mission/context,
- user role,
- collaborators,
- evidence,
- reflection,
- relevant development paths,
- verifier/mentor where appropriate,
- impact,
- next step.

It must support privacy tiers and child-safe defaults.

## 6. Programs

### PANSOFIE SCHOOL — primary market entry
School Experiences, teacher tooling, curriculum mapping, Passport, PANSOFIE DAY and impact reporting.

### PANSOFIE FAMILY
Family missions, guided reflection, shared Experiences and optional physical materials.

### PANSOFIE COMMUNITY
Municipalities, NGOs, seniors, local issues, volunteering and intergenerational cooperation.

### PANSOFIE YOUTH
Later-stage 15+ pathway for independence, finance, work, entrepreneurship, mentoring and civic projects.

`Generations` is a principle of Community, not a separate top-level program.

## 7. Labs

Labs are practical contexts, not separate companies.

- **LIFE** — health, food, finance, safety, everyday life.
- **MAKER** — craft, technology, AI, repair, manufacturing, prototyping.
- **NATURE** — microgreens, soil, water, biodiversity, food systems, circularity.
- **COMMUNITY** — seniors, neighbourhoods, culture, service, public life.
- **CHALLENGE** — sport, outdoor, tournaments, expeditions, team challenges.

## 8. PANSOFIE GO — Systems & Futures Lab

PANSOFIE GO is a flagship digital module, not a second master brand.

Core loop:

`PROBLÉM → FAKTA → PERSPEKTIVY → ROZHODNUTÍ → WHAT-IF DŮSLEDKY → TRADE-OFFS → REFLEXE`

Good use cases include AI in schools, park vs parking, water scarcity, local energy choices, food affordability vs health vs ecology, and automation/jobs.

AI may act as a Socratic guide: ask questions, expose missing perspectives and help reflection. AI must not become an authoritative judge of a person.

## 10. Safety & privacy

MUST:
- age-aware identity and consent;
- privacy by default;
- role-based visibility;
- no uncontrolled unknown-adult → child private communication;
- no sale of child data;
- no behavioural advertising to children;
- no emotion recognition in education;
- no hidden personality profiling;
- no one-number human score;
- auditable AI use;
- human accountability for consequential decisions.

## 11. Proof architecture

Every significant claim follows:

`CLAIM → SOURCE → HYPOTHESIS → METRIC → PILOT → RESULT → VALIDATED / REJECTED / UNKNOWN`

Each program maintains a Theory of Change:

`INPUT → ACTIVITIES → OUTPUTS → SHORT-TERM OUTCOMES → MID-TERM OUTCOMES → IMPACT HYPOTHESIS`

Do not convert an impact hypothesis into a marketing fact without evidence.

Pilot shape:
- 2–3 schools,
- 60–120 learners,
- 8 weeks,
- 3 major Experiences,

- teacher workload measured,
- student/teacher/parent outcomes measured,

## 14. PANSOFIE DAY

An evidence/community event where learners present results and failures, parents see real work, school shows impact, local actors see community value, partners can offer next Experiences, and PANSOFIE captures evidence for improvement.
