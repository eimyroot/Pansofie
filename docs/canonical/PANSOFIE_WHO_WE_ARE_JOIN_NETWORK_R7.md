# PANSOFIE WHO WE ARE / JOIN THE NETWORK R7

## Purpose

R7 closes the public-information gap between understanding Pansofie and knowing how to participate in it.

The public product must answer, truthfully and without invented operator/contact data:

1. Why does Pansofie exist?
2. What is Pansofie trying to change?
3. What is Pansofie today, and what is it not?
4. Who is needed for the first real validation?
5. How can a school, teacher, learner, family, mentor, partner, community or supporter contribute?
6. How can support create value for other people, not only for Pansofie?
7. What remains unavailable until a factual public contact/operator endpoint is approved?

## Public UX contract

### `/o-projektu`

The page becomes the human/project identity layer while preserving the existing maturity truth:

- exact public maturity headline remains visible;
- `FUNKČNÍ / TECHNICKY OVĚŘENÉ`, `TESTUJEME`, `JEŠTĚ NEPROBĚHLO`, and `DALŠÍ KROK` remain visible;
- it explains why Pansofie exists and what it is trying to change;
- it explicitly states what Pansofie is not;
- it does not invent team names, a legal operator, partners, a contact email or evidence of field impact;
- it names the roles currently needed for real-world validation.

### `/zapojit-se`

Default `/zapojit-se` becomes a public participation map, not a fake lead form.

It exposes eight contribution paths:

1. School
2. Teacher / educator
3. Young person
4. Family
5. Mentor / expert
6. Company / organization
7. Municipality / community
8. Supporter / connector

Each path must state:

- what the participant can bring;
- what their participation helps validate;
- the safest available next step.

The page also explains reciprocal value: knowledge, real problems, space/resources and verification can flow through the network to create value for learners, schools, communities and partners.

### PANSOFIEDIT compatibility

PANSOFIEDIT remains available at:

- `/zapojit-se?mode=simulator`
- `/zapojit-se?role=<supported-role>`

Legacy `/kontakt` must still resolve to URL `/zapojit-se` and open the PANSOFIEDIT journey without adding a fake form.

### Public navigation

Navigation and footer must expose:

- `O Pansofii`
- `Přidejte se`
- the existing 60-second PANSOFIEDIT CTA

## Truth boundaries

Unchanged:

- Experience remains the central system unit.
- Activity != Output != Adoption != Outcome != Impact.
- No human-worth score or child/person profiling.
- No unrestricted adult-child private messaging.
- Partner reviews bounded output against the Challenge brief, never human value.
- Experience Passport is private-by-default.
- Technical readiness is not evidence of pedagogical or field impact.
- A real school field pilot has not run yet.
- Willingness-to-pay is not proven.
- Public team/operator/legal/contact details must not be invented.

## Explicit current limitation

The public website still has no approved factual contact email, booking endpoint or lead-collection workflow. R7 therefore maps participation paths but does not simulate submission. Once a real public endpoint is approved, it can be added as a separate governed slice.

## Validation

Before merge:

- complete existing CI;
- R2-R6 browser regressions;
- R7 browser proof on desktop and mobile;
- no horizontal overflow;
- no runtime/console errors;
- `/zapojit-se` participation map visible and contains all eight paths;
- `/zapojit-se?mode=simulator` still exposes PANSOFIEDIT;
- role-deep-link still exposes PANSOFIEDIT;
- `/kontakt` compatibility preserved;
- `/o-projektu` preserves maturity truth and adds purpose/identity/participation content;
- no public form is introduced.

Production/deployment is outside this slice.
