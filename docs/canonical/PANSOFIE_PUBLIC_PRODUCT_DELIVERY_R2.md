# PANSOFIE Public Product Delivery R2

Canonical public-product delivery slice.

## Goal
Create one coherent public narrative and visual system for handoff/demo:

1. What Pansofie is
2. How one Experience works
3. Who participates and why
4. Role-specific responsibilities, value, access boundaries and next actions
5. What is ready now vs. being tested vs. planned
6. Trust, privacy and child-safety boundaries
7. Pilot entry and truthful next steps

## Non-negotiable truth boundaries
- Experience is the core unit.
- Activity is not Output; Output is not Adoption; Adoption is not Outcome; Outcome is not Impact.
- No human-worth score, person profiling, hireability prediction or public child profile.
- Partner reviews the bounded output against the Challenge brief, never the person.
- Family does not automatically receive private learner reflection.
- Mentor does not receive an unrestricted private child communication channel.
- Public simulator does not pretend to submit/store contact data.
- No invented legal entity, operator, contact email, calendar owner or canonical production domain.

## Public information architecture

### Home
Hero → Experience flow → role ecosystem/role infographic → method → maturity/status → growth paths → trust → pilot CTA.

### How it works
Experience definition → six-step lifecycle → role hand-offs → evidence/reflection/passport boundary → examples → CTA.

### Role infographics
Learner, Family, School, Mentor, Partner, Community all use the same information grammar:
- purpose
- does
- receives
- contributes
- can see
- cannot see
- safety boundary
- example Experiences
- next action

### Join / PANSOFIEDIT
Role-aware simulator → concrete result → truthful next action; no fake lead capture.

## Delivery scope
Public UX/content/design only. No production authorization, no new Supabase schema, no auth-policy change, no analytics provider, no legal placeholders, no invented domain.
