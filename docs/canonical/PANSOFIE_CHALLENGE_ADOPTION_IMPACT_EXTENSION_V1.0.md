Effective: 2026-08-17

## Purpose

This extension completes the final part of the canonical PANSOFIE loop:

`SPOLUPRÁCE → DOPAD`

It introduces a governed way for schools, municipalities, NGOs, companies and other verified organizations to contribute real problems, materials, expertise, facilities or funding that can become safe PANSOFIE Challenges.

PANSOFIE is not a corporate advertising channel to children. A partner is valuable because it contributes a real-world problem or capacity, not because its logo is visible.

## Global Challenge → Local Action

`GLOBÁLNÍ / SYSTÉMOVÝ PROBLÉM → LOKÁLNÍ PROJEV → OVĚŘENÝ PARTNER → CHALLENGE → TÝM → ŘEŠENÍ → PILOT → ADOPTION → OUTCOME → IMPACT → KNOWLEDGE BASE`

A global issue becomes educationally useful only when it can be translated into a bounded, locally actionable and safe problem.

Examples include waste and circularity, water, energy, biodiversity, food systems, digital exclusion, accessibility, social isolation, mobility, repair/reuse and responsible AI adoption.

- `PROBLEM_OWNER`
- `WHY`
- `LOCAL_CONTEXT`
- `BASELINE`
- `CONSTRAINTS`
- `TARGET_GROUP`
- `DEVELOPMENT_PATHS`
- `LABS`
- `RESOURCES`
- `SAFETY_CLASSIFICATION`
- `EXPECTED_OUTPUT`
- `EVIDENCE_RULES`
- `REFLECTION`
- `ADOPTION_PATH`
- `IMPACT_METRICS`

A Challenge that cannot define a safe and measurable local action remains an educational topic, not a PANSOFIE Challenge.

## Partner roles

- **CHALLENGE PARTNER** — provides a real problem and operational context.
- **MATERIAL PARTNER** — provides documented material streams for safe reuse, repair, recycling, composting or study.
- **KNOWLEDGE PARTNER** — provides experts and structured mentoring.
- **FACILITY PARTNER** — provides controlled access to relevant facilities.
- **ADOPTION PARTNER** — can evaluate and pilot a result.
- **FUNDING PARTNER** — funds access, facilitation, research or scale without buying child-data or advertising rights.

## Partner ethics boundary

MUST:
- no behavioural advertising to children;
- no sale or transfer of child data for commercial targeting;
- no unrestricted company employee → child private messaging;
- no requirement that learners promote a brand;
- no hidden product placement inside a Challenge;
- no raw child evidence/reflection access without a separately justified, lawful and safeguarded purpose;
- feedback addresses work/output, not learner human worth;
- material/facility Challenges require safety screening before publication.

Company involvement must create learning or public value that remains meaningful even if the company logo is removed.

## PANSOFIE Circular

`PANSOFIE CIRCULAR` is a cross-Lab vertical under **NATURE × MAKER × LIFE**. It is not a new top-level Lab or separate master brand.

Decision ladder:

`AVOID → REDUCE → REUSE → REPAIR → REPURPOSE → RECYCLE → COMPOST / BIOLOGICAL CYCLE → RECOVER → DISPOSE`

The correct pathway depends on material, contamination, safety, lifecycle evidence and local infrastructure.

Every Circular Challenge asks:
1. Can the waste stream be prevented?
2. Can less material be used?
3. Can the item/material be reused or repaired?
4. Can it be safely repurposed?
5. Is recycling technically and locally realistic?
6. Is biological treatment/composting actually appropriate for this material?
7. What residues remain?
8. What are the environmental and economic trade-offs?

### Material safety gate

No material enters a child-facing Challenge solely because a partner calls it recyclable, bio, natural or compostable.

Record where relevant:
- source/provenance;
- composition or uncertainty;
- contamination risk;
- sharp/thermal/electrical/chemical/biological hazards;
- PPE/supervision;
- legal handling constraints;
- end-of-life route;
- whether home/industrial compostability claims actually apply.

Unknown material risk defaults to `DO NOT DISTRIBUTE TO LEARNERS` until reviewed.

## Challenge lifecycle

`PROPOSED → SCREENING → ACCEPTED → DESIGNED → READY → ACTIVE → SUBMITTED → REVIEWED → PILOT_CANDIDATE → PILOTING → ADOPTED / NOT_ADOPTED → OUTCOME_TRACKING → CLOSED`

Terminal alternatives: `REJECTED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `BLOCKED`, `STOPPED_FOR_SAFETY`.

A learner Experience may complete even when the partner does not adopt the solution. Learning success and partner adoption are separate dimensions.

## Adoption Standard

Partner adoption is an independent organizational decision, not a learner score.

Levels:
- `REVIEWED`
- `INVITED_TO_REFINE`
- `PILOT_CANDIDATE`
- `PILOTING`
- `ADOPTED_PARTIAL`
- `ADOPTED`
- `NOT_ADOPTED`

## Impact Chain

PANSOFIE separates:

`ACTIVITY → OUTPUT → ADOPTION → OUTCOME → IMPACT`

Example:
- Activity: learners analyze a waste stream.
- Output: prototype/recommendation created.
- Adoption: partner pilots a changed process.
- Outcome: kilograms of material avoided or redirected.
- Impact: longer-term environmental/economic/social effect supported by evidence.

Do not report an output as impact.

Every impact record should carry baseline, metric definition, measurement period, data source, verifier, limitations and an attribution note.

## Pilot use

The first School pilot may include one bounded partner/community Challenge after safety and teacher-load review.

Recommended first vertical: a low-risk circularity/waste-prevention problem where material provenance is known, no hazardous processing is required, baseline can be measured, learners can create a real output in 2–4 weeks, and the partner can genuinely review the result.
