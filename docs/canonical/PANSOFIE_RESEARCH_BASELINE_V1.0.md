# PANSOFIE RESEARCH BASELINE V1.0

Status: CANONICAL RESEARCH BASELINE
Date: 2026-08-16

## Purpose

Records the reasoning baseline used to define PANSOFIE V1.0 and separates source-backed direction from hypotheses that require direct PANSOFIE validation.

## Supported strategic direction

### Historical/philosophical
- Comenius is a legitimate historical reference for connected/universal knowledge, harmony, education and broader human/social improvement.
- Modern PANSOFIE features are contemporary interpretations, not historical claims about Comenius.

### Education
- Current OECD/EU/Czech education direction emphasizes competencies, agency, critical thinking, cooperation, practical application, project work and lifelong learning.
- Real-world/project/service learning is a legitimate methodological family, but effect-size claims must remain cautious because evidence quality varies.
- Reflection is mandatory in PANSOFIE Experiences.

### Social need
- Youth wellbeing, belonging, social connection, resilience and meaningful participation are material contemporary concerns.
- PANSOFIE should avoid becoming another screen-retention product and instead use digital tools to trigger and document real-world activity.

### Competitive landscape
PANSOFIE does not own “holistic education”, “learning by doing”, “portfolio”, “projects”, “service” or “youth development”. Strong adjacent systems include:
- Scouting,
- DofE,
- Design for Change,
- JA Worldwide,
- Big Picture Learning.

Potential differentiation is the integrated combination of:
- Comenius-inspired connected/systemic thinking,
- real-world Experience Standard,
- evidence + reflection + transfer,
- school + family + community,
- longitudinal Passport,
- Czech/EU curriculum mapping,
- safe AI,
- local impact.

### Funding
Grant/foundation fit is plausible for pilot, research, access, inclusion, competencies, wellbeing, civic participation, digital/AI literacy and school-community cooperation.

Grant funding is not proof of product-market fit.

## Hypotheses requiring direct validation

- schools will pay for a PANSOFIE licence;
- exact acceptable annual price;
- teacher time saved/added;
- second-Experience/repeat rate;
- parent willingness to pay;
- school renewal intent;
- practical usefulness of the seven paths;
- proportionate evidence mechanisms;
- perceived long-term value of the Passport;
- measurable value of PANSOFIE GO for systems reasoning.

## Evidence discipline

Any numeric public claim must carry source, date, geographic scope, population definition, and a statement of what the statistic does and does not prove.

PANSOFIE-specific impact claims require PANSOFIE-specific data.
