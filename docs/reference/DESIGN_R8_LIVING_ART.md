# Pansofie R8 — Living Art + Gentle Participation

## Product rule
Pansofie offers opportunities; it does not manufacture obligations.

## Three pillars translated for today
- Vševěda / Pansofia — understand self, people and the world in context.
- Vševýchova / Pampaedia — grow throughout life and learn from one another.
- Všenáprava / Panorthosia — when something can be better, make a small useful change.

## Interaction rule
- Browse first; participation is optional.
- No human-worth score or leaderboard.
- No mandatory evidence/reflection flow.
- Completion can be a simple confirmation.
- Photos, notes and stories are voluntary.
- Reciprocity is circulation of value, not a 1:1 debt.

## Visual rule
Illustrations are compositional layers, not image cards:
- transparent/soft-keyed PNG art derived from the approved Pansofie mockup,
- CSS masking and mix-blend integration,
- artwork can bleed across card/section boundaries,
- semantic text and controls remain real HTML/React,
- no screenshot-as-page implementation.
