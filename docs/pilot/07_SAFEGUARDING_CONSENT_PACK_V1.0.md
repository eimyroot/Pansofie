# PANSOFIE SCHOOL — SAFEGUARDING & DATA PACK V1.0

Status: PILOT OPERATIONAL BASELINE
Legal status: NOT LEGAL ADVICE; controller-specific review is required before live school rollout.

## Core principle

Child safety and privacy are product requirements, not paperwork after launch.

## Mandatory roles before pilot

Assign named owners for:
- school pilot lead;
- safeguarding contact;
- data/privacy contact;
- technical incident contact;
- partner contact;
- PANSOFIE operator contact.

## Data minimization

Collect only what is necessary for authentication/role, school membership, assignment/run state, evidence, reflection, review, Passport and evaluation metrics. Avoid exact date of birth unless separately justified; use coarse age/grade where sufficient.

## Purpose separation

Do not use one global “parent consent” checkbox for everything.

Separate purposes may include school mission assignment, evidence review, reflection review, Passport review/view, research/evaluation, public photo/video publication and partner interaction. School/controller determines the lawful basis for each purpose.

## Guardian visibility

A guardian relationship by itself does not mean raw evidence/reflection is automatically visible.

Pilot default:
- Passport summary: possible under approved guardian view purpose;
- raw reflection: private unless separately justified;
- raw evidence: private unless separately justified.

## Publication

Participation must be separate from public marketing/photo publication. Prefer team/object/process visuals. Do not condition educational participation on marketing permission.

## Adult/partner communication

Not allowed: unrestricted private partner→child messaging, required exchange of personal phone/social contacts, or unsupervised private partner meetings.

Allowed: school-supervised sessions, moderated approved group channels and teacher-mediated feedback on work.

## Digital Bridge hard boundaries

No learner handling passwords/PINs, banking/payment, health records, identity documents, private messages or remote-access software.

## Incident ladder

- S0 observation — improvement opportunity.
- S1 near miss — stop/document/review controls.
- S2 safety/privacy incident — immediate notification to named school/PANSOFIE leads and controller process.
- S3 serious safeguarding concern — stop activity, protect child and escalate through school safeguarding procedures/applicable authorities.

## Age-appropriate transparency

Learner information should be plain-language, short, clear about what is collected/why/who sees it/what is optional, and make help visible.
