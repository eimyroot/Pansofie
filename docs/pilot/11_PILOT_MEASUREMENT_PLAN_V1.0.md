# PANSOFIE SCHOOL — PILOT MEASUREMENT PLAN V1.0

Status: PRE-REGISTERED PILOT DECISION FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

The pilot must be capable of proving PANSOFIE wrong. Measure desirability, feasibility, safety, learning-process quality, technical reliability and early commercial signal separately.

## Primary product metric

**Second Experience Rate (SER)**

`learners who start Experience 2 after completing/submitting Experience 1 / eligible Experience 1 completers`

A single activity may be novelty; re-engagement is a stronger repeat-value signal.

## Core metrics

### Participation funnel
assigned → started → evidence → reflection → submitted → revision → finalized → second Experience → third Experience.

### Learning-process
- evidence level E0–E4;
- reflection completeness;
- revision frequency;
- baseline + post-measurement rate;
- teams identifying a limitation/failure.

### Teacher feasibility
setup minutes, weekly overhead, review minutes/team, technical rescue cases, qualitative burden.

### Safety
S0/S1/S2/S3, material screen failures, unauthorized-access test failures.

Any serious safeguarding issue triggers immediate review regardless of other metrics.

### Stakeholder value
Learner: repeat intent, understood purpose, evidence reasoning.
Teacher: usefulness, fit, workload, repeat intent.
Parent/guardian: understood purpose, perceived value, privacy/safety confidence.
School leadership: continuation interest, payer/procurement path.
Partner: realism, usefulness, adoption status, repeat interest.

## Commercial signal

Do not treat survey positivity as willingness to pay. Record budget owner, purchase approver, procurement path, real paid-continuation conversation and objections.

## Pre-registered decision gates

These are hypotheses, not expected-result claims.

### GO candidate
- ≥70% active learners complete at least 2 of 3 Experiences;
- SER ≥60%;
- median teacher overhead outside normal lesson ≤30 min/week;
- no unresolved S2/S3 safety issue;
- no critical authorization/RLS failure;
- majority of teachers express repeat intent;
- at least one credible paid-continuation discussion;
- Challenge partner can separate adoption decision from learner assessment.

### CHANGE
- completion 40–69%; or
- SER 35–59%; or
- teacher overhead 31–60 min/week; or
- weak evidence/reflection despite completion; or
- unclear payer despite strong educational value.

### STOP / REDESIGN BEFORE SCALE
- completion <40%; or
- SER <35%; or
- median teacher overhead >60 min/week; or
- serious unresolved safeguarding problem; or
- repeated access-control failure; or
- routine sessions require founder intervention.

## Impact discipline

Output = completed solution/artifact.
Adoption = school/partner chooses to test/use it.
Outcome = predefined metric changes over defined period.
Impact = stronger attribution/duration/limitations required.

Never collapse these categories.
