# PANSOFIE SCHOOL PILOT — RESEARCH & ASSUMPTIONS V1.0

Status: EVIDENCE NOTE
Research snapshot: 2026-08-17

## Czech curriculum direction

The current revised RVP ZV competency framework defines eight key competencies. Relevant to PANSOFIE are especially problem solving, entrepreneurship/work, citizenship/sustainability, digital competence and personal/social competence.

Current official RVP language supports real problem identification and solution evaluation, practical project realization, active school/community participation, sustainability action, safe/socially useful digital technology use, reflection and formative feedback.

MŠMT postponed mandatory progressive implementation of the revised RVP ZV to 2028 onward. Mappings must be rechecked as RVP/ŠVP materials evolve.

## Formative feedback and portfolio

Current NPI materials for RVP implementation explicitly use evidence of learning, concrete formative feedback, self-assessment, work with mistakes and portfolio for tracking progress/reflection/next development.

PANSOFIE’s evidence + reflection + Passport direction is method-aligned, but PANSOFIE-specific effectiveness is unproven until pilot data exists.

## Project-based learning

A 2026 umbrella review of 15 PjBL meta-analyses found a reliably positive direction across several domains, but all included meta-analyses were rated Critically Low on AMSTAR 2. PANSOFIE should not market a guaranteed effect size.

## Reflection

A meta-analysis of 49 studies / 24,477 adolescents found community service effects were substantially stronger in studies including reflection than those without it. This supports making reflection structurally mandatory without proving every PANSOFIE reflection design will work.

## Child data

EDPB guidance treats children as requiring specific protection and emphasizes clear, easy-to-understand, age-appropriate information and privacy-protective age assurance.

Operational implications: data minimization, age-appropriate notices, purpose separation, privacy by default, no uncontrolled partner/adult direct channels.

## Circularity and waste

EU waste policy uses a waste hierarchy and prioritizes prevention before lower-order treatment. Hazardous waste has stricter controls and waste management must protect human health/environment.

Implications: AVOID/REDUCE before RECYCLE/COMPOST, known provenance, hazardous/unknown material excluded from child handling, and adoption/outcome separate from learning completion.

## Explicit pilot hypotheses

Not yet proven:
- schools will pay;
- target teacher load is achievable;
- three-Experience sequence has repeat value;
- Passport feels valuable;
- partner Challenges create useful adoption without commercializing children;
- a circular Challenge can produce measurable outcomes in an eight-week school pilot.
