# PANSOFIE SCHOOL — TEACHER PLAYBOOK V1.0

Status: PILOT PLAYBOOK
Audience: teacher / coordinator / school pilot lead

## Teacher role

The teacher is not expected to lecture PANSOFIE content. The teacher:
- frames the real problem;
- protects safety and fairness;
- helps teams narrow scope;
- asks reflection questions;
- gives formative feedback on work;
- verifies completion where appropriate;
- does not score a learner’s human worth, personality or “character”.

## Eight-week pilot rhythm

| Week | Main activity | Teacher task |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | onboarding | class setup, safety, parent information, accounts |
| 1 | Experience 1: discover | launch, team formation, baseline |
| 2 | Experience 1: act + reflect | facilitation, review, Passport |
| 3 | Experience 2: prepare | partner/senior safety brief, rehearsal |
| 4 | Experience 2: supervised action | supervise, debrief, review |
| 5 | Experience 3: challenge + baseline | partner brief, material safety gate |
| 6 | Experience 3: prototype/pilot | constraints, formative feedback |
| 7 | Experience 3: outcome + adoption | partner review, learner reflection |
| 8 | PANSOFIE DAY | evidence showcase + pilot feedback |

Schools may stretch the schedule. Do not compress safety preparation.

## Standard session structure

1. **Purpose — 5 min** — What real problem are we working on and why does it matter?
2. **Evidence check — 5–10 min** — What do we actually know? What is assumption?
3. **Team action — 25–50 min** — Learners plan, test, build, interview, measure or create.
4. **Reflection — 10–15 min** — What changed? What failed? What would we do differently?
5. **Next step — 5 min** — One explicit next action, owner and deadline.

## Formative feedback protocol

Use:
- **Daří se:** concrete evidence of what is working.
- **Ještě ne:** what is incomplete or unsupported.
- **Další krok:** one actionable next improvement.

Feedback addresses the work and process, not the learner’s value.

Avoid permanent labels such as “born leader” or “not technical”. Prefer evidence-based statements about the actual work.

## Team formation

Recommended team size: 3–5.

Use rotating roles:
- facilitator;
- evidence keeper;
- builder/doer;
- stakeholder contact;
- presenter.

No permanent “smart kid / weak kid” role.

## Evidence standard

Evidence should be proportionate and may include:
- before/after count or measurement;
- photo of an object/process without unnecessary people;
- prototype;
- diagram;
- short note;
- anonymized survey tally;
- partner/teacher confirmation;
- presentation or guide.

Never collect extra child data merely because the platform allows it.

## Reflection minimum

A complete reflection answers:
1. What did we actually do?
2. What evidence shows the result?
3. What failed or surprised us?
4. What did I/we learn?
5. Where could I/we use this again?
6. Who benefited, if anyone?
7. What should happen next?

## Inclusion

Every Experience must support:
- non-public contribution roles;
- oral or written reflection options where appropriate;
- team role choice;
- reasonable pacing;
- no forced disclosure of private family, health or psychological information;
- accessibility accommodations agreed by the school.

## Escalation

Stop the activity and escalate if:
- a child safety concern appears;
- a partner asks for private direct contact with learners;
- sensitive personal data is requested without approved purpose;
- a material’s provenance or safety is unclear;
- learners are asked to handle hazardous waste/equipment;
- filming/publication permission is unclear;
- a challenge becomes commercially promotional rather than educational.

## Teacher success criterion

PANSOFIE succeeds only if the teacher can run it without becoming a full-time project administrator. Teacher workload is measured as a product metric, not treated as “teacher resistance”.
