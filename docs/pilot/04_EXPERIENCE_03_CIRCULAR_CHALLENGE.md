# EXPERIENCE 03 — PANSOFIE CIRCULAR CHALLENGE

Status: PILOT EXPERIENCE
Recommended duration: 3 weeks
Risk class: VARIABLE — material safety screen is mandatory

## Driving question

**Jak můžeme u jednoho konkrétního materiálového toku snížit vznik odpadu nebo zvýšit jeho hodnotu, aniž bychom přeskočili bezpečnost, ekonomiku a skutečná data?**

## Canonical circular order

`AVOID → REDUCE → REUSE → REPAIR → REPURPOSE → RECYCLE → COMPOST/BIOLOGICAL CYCLE → RECOVER → DISPOSE`

Recycling is not automatically the first solution.

## Partner model

A school, municipality, NGO or company may serve as Challenge, Material, Knowledge, Facility, Adoption or Funding Partner. Payment does not buy a positive result or access to child data.

## Recommended first pilot streams

Prefer known, low-risk streams:
- clean paper/cardboard;
- clean packaging offcuts;
- untreated known wood offcuts;
- clean textile offcuts;
- school single-use packaging counts;
- preventable paper/printing waste.

Conditional, adult-managed:
- coffee grounds / food scraps for supervised compost observation;
- garden bio-material with known provenance.

Do not use unknown mixed waste.

## Process

1. Problem owner brief — what material/resource problem actually exists?
2. Provenance — identify source and composition as far as reasonably possible.
3. Safety classification — GREEN / YELLOW / RED.
4. Baseline — mass, count, frequency or cost proxy.
5. Hierarchy check — can we avoid/reduce before recycle/compost?
6. Ideas — at least 3 interventions.
7. Trade-off check — safety, resources, cost, practicality, unintended effects.
8. Prototype/pilot — bounded real test.
9. Re-measure — same metric where possible.
10. Partner review — adopt / partial / refine / not adopt, with reason.
11. Reflection — learning result separate from business/environmental result.
12. Impact status — output only unless outcome data exists.

## Required evidence

- material/problem brief;
- provenance statement;
- safety screen;
- baseline;
- solution/prototype;
- post-pilot measurement;
- partner adoption decision;
- reflection.

## Material safety invariant

Unknown, hazardous, contaminated or legally controlled waste is not distributed to learners.

## RVP anchors

- KOB-UDR-000-ZV9-001 — takes steps toward sustainability based on consideration of possible future scenarios.
- KPP — opportunities, resources, project implementation and teamwork.
- KRP — problem analysis and solution evaluation.
- KOB — local/global interconnectedness, responsible action and participation.

## Valid pilot wording

Valid: “Teams reduced use of disposable cups during the 5-day test from baseline X to Y; school chose to extend the test for one month.”

Invalid without longer evidence: “PANSOFIE solved the school’s waste problem.”
