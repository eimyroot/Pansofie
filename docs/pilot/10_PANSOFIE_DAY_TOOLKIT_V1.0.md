# PANSOFIE DAY TOOLKIT V1.0

Status: PILOT EVENT FORMAT
Purpose: evidence showcase, reflection and next-step bridge — not a talent show.

## Recommended format

Duration: 75–100 minutes
Audience: learners, teachers, invited parents/guardians, school leadership, approved community/partner guests.

## Space

Use stations rather than one long stage program:
1. PROBLÉM — what we observed.
2. CO JSME ZKUSILI — prototype/action.
3. DŮKAZ — what changed / what did not.
4. CO SELHALO — normalized failure/learning.
5. CO DÁL — adoption or next Experience.

## Team display template

Every team displays problem/question, baseline, action/prototype, evidence, reflection, next step and impact status `OUTPUT / PILOT / ADOPTION / OUTCOME` — not inflated “impact”.

## Partner feedback

Partner may answer:
- What was useful?
- What assumption was wrong?
- Would you test/adopt/refine this?
- What constraint did learners miss?

Partner does not rank children or recruit based on private learner data.

## No leaderboard

Do not award “best child”. Optional recognition can address work categories such as strongest evidence, best iteration after failure, clearest explanation, strongest stakeholder listening or safest practical solution. These are event feedback, not permanent human scores.

## Privacy

Use an approved public display zone. Non-public reflection stays private. Photography rules are announced and identifiable child publication is handled separately.

## Evidence capture

May include number of presenting teams, completed Experiences, partner adoption decisions, anonymous attendee feedback and teacher observations. Do not infer long-term impact from attendance.

## Closing question

**Jaký je náš další skutečný krok?**
