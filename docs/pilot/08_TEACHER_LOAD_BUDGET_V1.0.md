# PANSOFIE — TEACHER LOAD BUDGET V1.0

Status: PILOT PRODUCT METRIC

## Principle

Teacher workload is a product constraint. If PANSOFIE creates excessive hidden teacher work, the product has failed.

## Target budget

These are internal hypotheses to validate, not universal evidence-based limits.

### One-time setup
Target: ≤90 minutes per teacher after central coordinator setup.

### Weekly overhead outside normal teaching
Target median: ≤30 minutes per teacher/week.

### Review target
Target: ≤5 minutes per team per Experience for standard completion review.

Safeguarding/revision exceptions are tracked separately and must not be hidden in averages.

## Platform should support

- assignment status;
- completion queue;
- evidence/reflection collation;
- standard review prompts;
- Passport creation after governed finalization;
- export of pilot metrics.

It should not automate consequential judgement about learner worth.

## Weekly measurement

Teacher records prep, admin, review, partner coordination, technical support minutes and free-text friction.

## Redesign triggers

AMBER:
- median overhead >30 min/week for two weeks.

RED:
- median overhead >60 min/week;
- repeated duplicate data entry;
- >10% runs require technical/manual rescue;
- teachers need unofficial side spreadsheets to understand state.

RED blocks scaling until redesigned.
