# EXPERIENCE 02 — DIGITÁLNÍ MOST

Status: PILOT EXPERIENCE
Recommended duration: 2 weeks
Risk class: MEDIUM because it involves intergenerational contact and digital privacy

## Driving question

**Jak můžeme někomu z jiné generace bezpečně usnadnit jednu konkrétní digitální činnost?**

## Purpose

The Experience connects digital competence with empathy, communication and real service. It is not a generic “teach seniors technology” campaign.

## PANSOFIE paths

Primary:
- Poznání & myšlení
- Vztahy & spolupráce
- Občanství & přínos

Secondary:
- Tvorba & řešení problémů
- Samostatnost & podnikavost

## Allowed pilot tasks

Prefer bounded low-risk tasks such as:
- using maps/navigation;
- making a video call;
- sharing a photo safely;
- QR code basics;
- adjusting text size/accessibility;
- recognizing common scam/phishing signals using prepared examples;
- organizing photos/files;
- basic privacy settings using a demo or the adult’s own supervised device.

## Excluded in pilot

Learners must not:
- receive or type another person’s passwords/PINs;
- handle banking or payment;
- log into eGovernment or health records on behalf of an adult;
- store identity documents;
- install remote-control software;
- access another person’s private messages;
- provide cybersecurity guarantees;
- contact the adult privately outside the supervised activity.

## Safeguarding model

- partner/participant identity known to the school;
- activity supervised by school/approved adult;
- no unrestricted one-to-one private messaging;
- no exchange of child personal contact details;
- adult owns their device and credentials;
- learner may demonstrate but should not take control of sensitive accounts;
- photo/video publication requires a separate approved basis/permission.

## Process

1. Need discovery — ask the adult what single task would help.
2. Rephrase — learner confirms the goal in plain language.
3. Risk screen — check whether task enters excluded areas.
4. Prepare — create a 3–7 step guide.
5. Teach — adult performs the task; learner guides.
6. Check understanding — adult repeats independently if comfortable.
7. Improve guide — revise based on what was confusing.
8. Reflect — what did the learner assume incorrectly? what communication worked?
9. Evidence — guide + anonymized completion note + reflection.

## Required evidence

- one co-designed guide/checklist;
- teacher/approved partner confirmation that supervised session occurred;
- learner reflection;
- no password, credential or sensitive adult data in evidence.

## RVP anchors

- KDI-ZAP-000-ZV9-001 — thoughtful sharing/use of digital information for community/team purposes.
- KDI-BZK-000-ZV9-001 — preventing situations threatening device/data or physical/mental health.
- KDI-VIN-000-ZV9-001 — using digital technologies to simplify workflows or improve work outcomes.
- KOS — empathy, relationships and reflective social learning.

## Adoption possibility

A senior centre/library/community partner may adopt the learner-created guide for repeated use. Adoption is recorded separately from learner completion.
