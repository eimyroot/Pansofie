# EXPERIENCE 01 — ZLEPŠI SVOU ŠKOLU

Status: PILOT EXPERIENCE
Recommended duration: 2 weeks
Team size: 3–5
Risk class: LOW, if bounded to approved reversible changes

## Driving question

**Co můžeme během dvou týdnů ve škole skutečně zlepšit a doložit, že se něco změnilo?**

## PANSOFIE paths

Primary:
- Tvorba & řešení problémů
- Občanství & přínos
- Vztahy & spolupráce
- Samostatnost & podnikavost

Secondary:
- Poznání & myšlení

## Mission

Find one bounded school-life problem, establish a baseline, propose multiple solutions, choose one safe feasible intervention, test it and evaluate the result.

## Constraints

Allowed examples:
- confusing recycling labels;
- noisy transition process;
- unused shared space;
- wasted paper;
- unclear wayfinding;
- lost-property process;
- class resource organization;
- school micro-event or shared-space improvement.

Not allowed without separate school approval:
- structural/building changes;
- electrical work;
- security/access changes;
- disciplinary actions against named individuals;
- collection of sensitive personal data;
- publication of identifiable complaints about staff/learners.

## Process

1. Observe — list 5 possible problems.
2. Select — choose one with an impact/effort/safety screen.
3. Baseline — count, map or document the current state.
4. Listen — obtain 3–8 short stakeholder inputs without unnecessary identity data.
5. Generate — create at least 3 solution options.
6. Choose — justify one solution against evidence and constraints.
7. Act — run a reversible pilot.
8. Measure — repeat the baseline measure where possible.
9. Reflect — document result, failure, transfer and next step.
10. Share — present the work, including uncertainty.

## Required evidence

- baseline;
- problem statement;
- one solution artifact/prototype;
- result evidence;
- reflection.

Optional: anonymized stakeholder tally, environment/object photo, teacher verification.

## Success

A traceable chain:
`Problem → baseline → decision → action → evidence → reflection`

The intervention may fail. A well-evidenced failed test still counts as a valuable Experience.

## RVP anchors

- KRP — problem identification, information gathering, analysis and solution evaluation.
- KPP-REA-000-ZV9-001 — practical realization of activities/projects.
- KOB-PCP-000-ZV9-001 — direct action to influence change in local surroundings while respecting others’ rights/interests.

## Teacher load target

- launch preparation: ≤30 min once;
- weekly coordination outside normal lesson: ≤20 min;
- review: ≤5 min per team plus exceptions.

These are internal pilot design targets to validate, not evidence-based universal limits.
