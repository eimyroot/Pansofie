Status: CURRICULUM MAPPING HYPOTHESIS
Reference snapshot: 2026-08-17
Target: revised RVP ZV, 2nd stage / outputs toward grade 9

## Important limitation

This crosswalk is a PANSOFIE interpretation, not an official certification by MŠMT/NPI.

The revised RVP ZV remains in a transition period. Mandatory rollout was postponed to start progressively from 2028, and MŠMT announced further simplification/formal adjustments. Revalidate the mapping before every contracted pilot and reconcile with the school’s currently effective ŠVP.

## Strong competency anchors

### KRP — Klíčová kompetence k řešení problémů
PANSOFIE fit: identifies real problems, gathers relevant information, compares solutions, plans action and evaluates effectiveness.

Primary: `ZLEPŠI SVOU ŠKOLU`, `CIRCULAR CHALLENGE`.

### KPP — Klíčová kompetence k podnikavosti a pracovní
Current RVP framing emphasizes practical experience with planning and realizing activities/projects.

Specific anchor:
- `KPP-REA-000-ZV9-001` — learner realizes activities according to own or group procedures.

Primary: all three, strongest in school improvement and circular challenge.

### KOB — Klíčová kompetence k občanství a udržitelnosti
Current RVP framing explicitly connects this competency with lived experience, active participation, local/community context and reflection.

Specific anchors:
- `KOB-PCP-000-ZV9-001` — purposeful direct action influencing changes in local/wider surroundings while respecting rights/interests of others.
- `KOB-UDR-000-ZV9-001` — takes steps toward sustainability after considering possible future scenarios.

### KDI — Klíčová kompetence digitální
Specific anchors:
- `KDI-ZAP-000-ZV9-001` — thoughtful sharing of data/information for personal growth, school/community support or team project work.
- `KDI-BZK-000-ZV9-001` — prevents situations threatening device/data or physical/mental health.
- `KDI-VIN-000-ZV9-001` — uses digital technology to simplify workflows and improve outcomes.
- `KDI-TDO-000-ZV9-001` — creates digital content in formats that strengthen outputs of own creative work.

Primary: `DIGITÁLNÍ MOST`.

### KOS — Klíčová kompetence osobnostní a sociální
PANSOFIE fit: self-reflection, empathy, collaboration, relationship building and resilience after failure/revision.

## Mapping discipline

A teacher should select only the RVP outcomes actually evidenced by the task. Do not map every mission to every competency for marketing effect.

For each mapped outcome state:
- intended competency;
- learner action expected;
- observable evidence;
- reflection prompt;
- whether evidence is sufficient to discuss progress.
