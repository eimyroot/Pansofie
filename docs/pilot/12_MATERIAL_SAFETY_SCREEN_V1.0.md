# PANSOFIE CIRCULAR — MATERIAL SAFETY SCREEN V1.0

Status: MANDATORY FOR CHILD-FACING MATERIAL CHALLENGES

## Principle

“Recyclable”, “natural”, “bio” or “compostable” does not mean safe for learner handling. Unknown material defaults to `DO NOT DISTRIBUTE` until reviewed.

## Required provenance

- owner/source;
- description;
- known composition;
- previous use/contact;
- contamination possibility;
- treatment/coating/chemical history if relevant;
- storage condition;
- proposed learner activity;
- adult supervisor;
- disposal/return plan.

## Classification

### GREEN — normal supervised classroom material
Examples: clean paper/cardboard, new/clean packaging offcuts, untreated verified craft wood, clean textile offcuts. Still check allergy/sharp-edge/fire/use context.

### YELLOW — specialist/adult review required
Examples: food scraps/coffee grounds for compost, garden material, unknown coatings/adhesives, materials requiring cutting/heating/tools, dusty/mold-prone material, partner production offcuts without complete composition documentation.

### RED — no child handling in standard pilot
Examples: batteries, e-waste internals, chemicals/solvents, medical waste, contaminated packaging, unknown industrial waste, asbestos/construction debris, treated/painted unknown wood, sharp/pressurized/toxic/flammable materials, hazardous-waste classification.

## Compost-specific controls

Prefer system design/measurement over direct handling of decomposing waste. If food/coffee-ground compost observation is used: adult-managed collection, known source, no moldy/unknown waste, allergy/hygiene check, PPE/handwashing as required by school risk assessment, no tasting and no enclosed-space decomposition exposure.

## Stop conditions

Stop if provenance cannot be confirmed; unexpected odor/dust/leakage/biological growth/sharp contamination appears; partner changes material batch/composition; or activity requires unapproved equipment.

## Evidence

Store the screen as Challenge evidence. It does not replace competent risk assessment where one is required.
