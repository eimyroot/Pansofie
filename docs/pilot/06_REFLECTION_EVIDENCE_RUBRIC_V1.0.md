# PANSOFIE — REFLECTION & EVIDENCE RUBRIC V1.0

Status: PILOT RUBRIC

## Principle

PANSOFIE distinguishes activity from learning. A learner can be busy without understanding.

## Evidence quality levels

### E0 — CLAIM ONLY
“I think it worked.” No supporting evidence.

### E1 — TRACE
A note/photo/artifact shows activity occurred.

### E2 — RESULT EVIDENCE
Evidence supports a concrete result or completed output.

### E3 — COMPARATIVE EVIDENCE
Before/after, baseline/re-measurement, independent confirmation or another comparison supports a change claim.

### E4 — OUTCOME EVIDENCE
Follow-up evidence supports an outcome over time.

For the school pilot, most learner Experiences should aim for E2–E3. E4 is not required for learning completion.

## Reflection rubric

Use four levels per dimension: `NOT YET / EMERGING / CLEAR / TRANSFERABLE`.

Dimensions:
1. What happened? — distinguish action from result.
2. Evidence reasoning — explain why evidence supports/does not support a claim.
3. Learning from failure/change — identify difficulty, adaptation and principle learned.
4. Perspective/collaboration — show another stakeholder’s perspective and trade-offs.
5. Transfer — name a plausible new context and next action.

## Teacher feedback

Do not calculate one “PANSOFIE score” for the learner.

Feedback should identify:
- one strength;
- one evidence/reflection gap;
- one next action.

## Completion rule

An Experience may be completed even if the intervention failed, provided that:
- task was carried out safely;
- minimum evidence exists;
- reflection is substantive;
- teacher review confirms process requirements.

## Passport language

Record what the learner/team did and evidenced. Avoid permanent trait labels such as “leader”, “creative type”, or “low resilience”.
