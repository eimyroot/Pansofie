# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — PARTNER PORTAL SPECIFICATION V1.0

Status: PRE-IMPLEMENTATION UX/DATA SPEC

## Product goal
Give verified partner adults the minimum information/actions required to operate a Challenge without exposing private learner data.

## Navigation
`Dashboard → Challenges → Challenge Detail → Submitted Solutions → Feedback → Pilot/Adoption → Outcome Tracking → Reports → Organisation/People`

## Dashboard
Active Challenges, next required action, moderated sessions, review queue, pilot/adoption status and outcome measurement due dates.

## Challenge Detail
Visible: approved brief, baseline/constraints/resources, expected output, timeline, safety summary, approved experts and aggregated team status.

Not visible by default: private reflection, unrelated learner profile data, raw evidence outside approved review purpose or guardian information.

## Solution review
Partner can record `REVIEWED / INVITED_TO_REFINE / PILOT_CANDIDATE / NOT_ADOPTED` with reason and bounded feedback.

## Pilot/adoption
Record decision, scope, period, owner, metric, baseline and measurement period.

## Outcome report labels
`OUTPUT ONLY / ADOPTED / OUTCOME OBSERVED / IMPACT NOT YET ESTABLISHED`.

## Authorization principle
Partner permissions are organization-, Challenge- and purpose-scoped. “Partner” is never a global right to browse learners.
