# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — FIRST PARTNER PILOT PROTOCOL V1.0

Status: RECOMMENDED FIRST COMMERCIAL/IMPACT VALIDATION

## Objective
Validate that one real partner can supply a useful problem and participate in review/adoption without increasing learner risk or teacher burden beyond the pilot budget.

## Recommended Challenge
Low-risk circularity/waste-prevention Challenge with known provenance. Prefer clean paper/cardboard, clean packaging offcuts, verified untreated craft wood, clean textile offcuts or preventable printing/single-use resource waste.

Avoid hazardous waste, e-waste internals, chemicals, unknown industrial mixtures and sensitive customer data in the first pilot.

## Partner commitments
One accountable Challenge owner, completed brief, baseline data, provenance where relevant, maximum 1–2 approved expert sessions, timely response to outputs, explicit adoption/refinement/not-adopted decision and willingness to re-measure one metric if adopted.

## Suggested timeline
Week 0 verification/screening; Week 1 discovery/baseline; Week 2 ideas/trade-offs/prototype; Week 3 bounded pilot/partner review; Week 4+ outcome measurement if adopted.

## Success conditions
No unresolved safety/privacy issue; teacher overhead within budget target; real baseline/constraints; real adoption decision; learner completion independent of adoption; at least one paid-continuation discussion; outcome measurable if adopted.

## Stop conditions
Stop/redesign if partner seeks marketing access to children, lacks safe provenance, demands sensitive/confidential handling beyond the model, requires guaranteed positive claims or makes the Challenge operationally impossible for school.
