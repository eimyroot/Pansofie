# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — PARTNER AGREEMENT SKELETON V1.0

Status: DRAFT CONTRACT SKELETON — REQUIRES CZECH LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE SIGNATURE

This is a product requirements document, not legal advice or a ready-to-sign contract.

## Required sections
1. Parties and Challenge purpose.
2. Scope/deliverables: partner resources, PANSOFIE facilitation, school participation, review/adoption/reporting.
3. Safety/safeguarding: supervision, communication and material/facility controls.
4. Data protection: actual controller/processor roles, purpose limitation, no default private-reflection access, no child-data marketing profile, retention/deletion.
5. Confidentiality: partner pre-classifies information as public, challenge-safe or confidential/not shareable. Redesign rather than make broad learner NDAs the normal route.
6. Intellectual property: payment does not automatically transfer learner/team IP. Commercial use requires separate rights assessment/agreement.
7. Branding/publicity: no compulsory learner promotion; media consent separate from participation.
8. Adoption/impact: no unsupported impact claims.
9. Fees/cancellation: fee, costs, schedule, postponement and safety cancellation.
10. Liability/insurance/facility rules: completed for actual deployment after legal/insurance review.
11. Termination: immediate suspension for safeguarding/privacy/safety/integrity breach.
12. Evidence/audit: retain verification, safety, review, adoption and outcome evidence.
