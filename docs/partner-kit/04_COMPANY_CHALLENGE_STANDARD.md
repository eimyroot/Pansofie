# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — COMPANY CHALLENGE STANDARD V1.0

Status: OPERATIONAL STANDARD

## Required Challenge fields
PROBLEM_OWNER, WHY, LOCAL_CONTEXT, BASELINE, CONSTRAINTS, TARGET_GROUP, DEVELOPMENT_PATHS, LABS, RESOURCES, MATERIAL/FACILITY SAFETY, EXPECTED_OUTPUT, EVIDENCE_RULES, REFLECTION, PARTNER REVIEW MODE, ADOPTION_PATH, OUTCOME_METRIC, MEASUREMENT_PERIOD and EXIT/STOP CONDITIONS.

## Lifecycle
`PROPOSED → SCREENING → ACCEPTED → DESIGNED → READY → ACTIVE → SUBMITTED → REVIEWED → PILOT_CANDIDATE → PILOTING → ADOPTED / NOT_ADOPTED → OUTCOME_TRACKING → CLOSED`

Alternative exits: `REJECTED / WITHDRAWN / BLOCKED / STOPPED_FOR_SAFETY`.

## Acceptance gate
A Challenge must be real/bounded, safe enough for target age, educationally meaningful, measurable at least at output level, possible without unnecessary child data, compatible with teacher workload, feasible within declared resources/time, non-exploitative and clear about what the partner may actually adopt.

## Feedback model
Partner may evaluate feasibility, clarity, usefulness, constraints missed and readiness for refinement/pilot. Partner does not evaluate learner worth, personality or predicted potential.

## Completion vs adoption
A learner/team may successfully complete the Experience even when the partner chooses `NOT_ADOPTED`.

## Outcome rule
An adopted solution becomes an outcome candidate only when the same defined metric can be measured after a defined period. Output is never auto-promoted to impact.
