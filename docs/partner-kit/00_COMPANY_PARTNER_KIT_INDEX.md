# PANSOFIE × FIRMY / PARTNERS KIT V1.0

Status: PRE-DEPLOYMENT PARTNER PRODUCT KIT
Date: 2026-08-17

## Purpose
Turn the canonical `Challenge → Adoption → Outcome → Impact` layer into a partner-ready product without turning learners into unpaid commercial labour, advertising targets or a source of private data.

## Product promise
A partner brings a real bounded problem, capability or resource. PANSOFIE converts it into a safe, educationally meaningful Challenge. Learners create evidence-backed work and reflection. The partner may review the work and, if appropriate, test or adopt a solution under separate governed terms.

## Golden path
`VERIFIED PARTNER → CHALLENGE INTAKE → SCREENING → DESIGN → SCHOOL MATCH → DELIVERY → SOLUTION REVIEW → PILOT DECISION → ADOPTION / NOT ADOPTED → OUTCOME TRACKING → REPORT`

13. Partner measurement/commercial validation
14. Research/legal assumptions note

## Non-negotiables
- payment never buys a positive learner assessment or impact claim;
- no commercial targeting using child data;
- no unrestricted partner employee → learner private messaging;
- no automatic commercial license to learner/team outputs;
- participation is not conditioned on marketing/photo consent;
- partner feedback evaluates the work, not human worth;
- unknown/hazardous material is not distributed to learners;
- output, adoption, outcome and impact remain separate.

## Deploy boundary
This kit is documentation/product governance. It does not create partner database entities, billing, a production Partner Portal or live deployment by itself.
