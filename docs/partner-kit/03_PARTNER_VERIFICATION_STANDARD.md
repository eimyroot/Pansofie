# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — PARTNER VERIFICATION STANDARD V1.0

Status: REQUIRED BEFORE CHILD-FACING PARTICIPATION

## Verification objective
Know who the partner is, what role it will play, which adults may participate and whether the proposed problem/resource is suitable for the target age and school context.

## Minimum organisation record
- legal/common name;
- registration/identity information appropriate to entity type;
- website/contact domain;
- named accountable partner owner;
- billing/contact details where commercial;
- partner role(s);
- challenge purpose;
- declared conflicts/regulated context where relevant.

## Adult participant record
For each employee/expert who interacts with learners record full name, organisation role, PANSOFIE role, approved interaction mode, safeguarding briefing, school-required approvals and authorization period.

## Verification outcomes
`PENDING → VERIFIED / VERIFIED_WITH_CONTROLS / REJECTED / SUSPENDED / EXPIRED`

## Automatic escalation
Escalate when a Challenge involves high-stakes services, vulnerable persons, confidential industrial data, hazardous/unknown materials, unsupervised facility access, direct digital communication with learners, strong marketing/sales objective or unnecessary personal-data collection.

## Partner exclusion examples
Reject/suspend partners or Challenges requiring hidden advertising/product placement, child-data commercial profiling, private unsupervised adult-child contact, unsafe materials/facilities, exploitative tasks, predetermined positive outcomes or misleading impact claims.
