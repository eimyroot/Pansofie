# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — PARTNER MEASUREMENT & COMMERCIAL VALIDATION V1.0

Status: PRE-REGISTERED VALIDATION FRAMEWORK

## Dimensions
Measure partner desirability, school/teacher feasibility, learner educational value, safety/privacy integrity, Challenge realism, adoption utility, outcome measurability and willingness to pay/repeat separately.

## Partner metrics
Challenge screen outcome, prep minutes, expert minutes, review timeliness, usefulness with reason, adoption state, outcome metric availability, repeat intent, paid continuation conversation, accepted/rejected price band and procurement blockers.

## GO candidate after first bounded partner pilot
- usable baseline/constraints;
- no safety/privacy breach;
- real adoption decision;
- partner distinguishes learner success from business adoption;
- teacher overhead acceptable;
- at least one credible paid repeat/next-Challenge conversation;
- if adopted, measurable outcome period agreed.

## CHANGE
Strong educational but unclear partner value; partner likes but will not fund; review burden high; Challenge too broad; excessive direct learner access needed; reporting does not fit partner decisions.

## STOP/REDESIGN
Model depends on marketing to children, free commercial IP transfer, repeated safeguarding/data boundary pressure, unsafe material/facility or fabricated/guaranteed impact claims.

## Commercial evidence rule
A positive comment is not willingness to pay. Record actual budget discussion, procurement owner and concrete next-step acceptance/rejection.
