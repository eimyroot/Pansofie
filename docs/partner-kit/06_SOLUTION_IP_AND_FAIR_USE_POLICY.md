# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — SOLUTION / IP & FAIR USE POLICY V1.0

Status: PRODUCT POLICY — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED FOR COMMERCIAL LICENSING

## Default
A partner does **not** acquire ownership or a commercial exploitation licence to learner/team outputs merely by funding or proposing a Challenge.

## Czech school-work context
Czech Copyright Act No. 121/2000 Sb. contains a specific regime for `školní dílo` in §60. Whether a specific Challenge output qualifies, who must consent and how a licence may be granted depends on the actual facts. PANSOFIE therefore does not auto-assign rights through platform or standard Challenge terms.

## Allowed by default
Partner may review submitted output inside the governed process, give feedback, decide whether to invite refinement/pilot and reference aggregated/anonymized programme facts under the publicity policy.

## Commercial-use gate
Before commercial use of a specific learner/team-created asset, prototype, design, text, code or other protectable output:
1. identify authors/contributors;
2. determine school-work/rights status;
3. determine embedded third-party material;
4. assess age/legal-capacity requirements;
5. define licence/use scope, term, territory and compensation if appropriate;
6. obtain required written agreements/approvals;
7. store decision evidence.

## Fairness rule
Educational participation is not free commercial labour. If an output creates material commercial value, escalate to a separate fair-use/licensing discussion.

## Attribution
Public attribution is opt-in and separate from participation. Where inappropriate, use team/project attribution or anonymized reporting.

## AI-assisted outputs
Record material AI assistance when relevant to rights/provenance and do not represent AI-generated material as solely original learner authorship.

**Operational invariant: no automatic partner IP transfer. Commercial use is a separate governed/legal step.**
