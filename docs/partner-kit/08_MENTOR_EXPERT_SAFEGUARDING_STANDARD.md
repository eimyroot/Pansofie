# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — MENTOR / EXPERT SAFEGUARDING STANDARD V1.0

Status: REQUIRED FOR PARTNER ADULTS INTERACTING WITH LEARNERS

## Role
A partner expert supports a Challenge with domain knowledge, constraints and feedback. They are not a private coach, recruiter, therapist or evaluator of learner worth.

## Preferred interaction modes
- teacher/PANSOFIE-moderated group session;
- scheduled facility visit with school supervision;
- approved Q&A routed through platform/school;
- team-level feedback visible to the responsible adult.

## Prohibited by default
- no unrestricted partner employee → learner private messaging;
- moving conversations to personal messaging/social accounts;
- requesting unneeded personal contact details;
- gifts/incentives tied to private contact or promotion;
- recruitment promises outside school-approved process;
- requests for sensitive/private family information.

## Expert briefing
Confirm Challenge scope, age group, communication rules, data/privacy limits, feedback model, escalation contact, timing and supervision before participation.

## Incident response
Any boundary concern is logged and escalated to the named school/PANSOFIE safeguarding contact. Partner access can be suspended immediately pending review.
