## Child data
EDPB guidance states children receive specific GDPR protection and organisations should use clear age-appropriate information and extra care. PANSOFIE therefore keeps partner access purpose-scoped and privacy-by-default.

Sources:
- https://www.edpb.europa.eu/topics/key-gdpr-concepts/children_en
- https://www.edpb.europa.eu/topics/ai-and-technology/privacy-by-design-and-by-default_en

## Advertising to children
Annex I point 28 of Directive 2005/29/EC prohibits direct exhortations in advertising telling children to buy products or persuade adults to buy. PANSOFIE uses a stricter internal rule: partner participation must not become compulsory child-facing sales promotion.

Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex:32005L0029

## School work / IP
Czech Copyright Act No. 121/2000 Sb. contains a specific `školní dílo` regime in §60. Current official text is available through e-Sbírka/Ministry of Culture. A standard PANSOFIE contract must not pretend company funding automatically resolves rights to school outputs.

Sources:
- https://www.mk.gov.cz/autorsky-zakon-cs-49
- https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2000/121

Operational conclusion: **no automatic partner IP transfer**. Commercial use is a separate governed/legal step.

## Waste/material challenges
EU Waste Framework Directive establishes the waste hierarchy and stricter controls for hazardous waste; the 2025 targeted revision entered into force in October 2025. PANSOFIE prioritises prevention/reuse before recycling and treats unknown/hazardous streams conservatively.

Source: https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/waste-framework-directive_en
