# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — BRANDING, PUBLICITY & MEDIA POLICY V1.0

Status: REQUIRED PARTNER BOUNDARY

## Principle
Educational participation and public promotion are separate choices.

## Allowed partner visibility
Subject to agreed terms: partner named by role, factual Challenge description, partner logo in adult-facing partner/report areas and aggregated programme outcomes supported by evidence.

## Not allowed by default
- required learner brand advocacy;
- hidden product placement;
- calls to children to purchase or persuade parents to purchase;
- ad targeting based on learner data;
- using learner names/images/testimonials as consideration for access;
- implying endorsement where none exists.

## Photo/video/story consent
Public media permission is separate from core educational processing. A learner/team may participate without being used in marketing/publicity.

## Claims
Use `ACTIVITY → OUTPUT → ADOPTION → OUTCOME → IMPACT`. Do not publish impact when only activity/output occurred.

## Regulatory baseline
EU unfair-commercial-practices rules prohibit direct exhortations in advertising telling children to buy or persuade adults to buy. PANSOFIE adopts a stricter brand-neutral child-facing default regardless of whether a specific placement would meet that legal test.
