# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — PRODUCT DEFINITION & VALUE PROPOSITION V1.0

Status: PRODUCT BASELINE

## What the product is
PANSOFIE × FIRMY is a governed bridge between real organisational problems and real-world learning.

The company is not a sponsor inserted into a classroom. It is a **problem/capability partner** that can contribute a real bounded challenge, safe material/resource stream, expert knowledge, facility access, adoption pathway or funding.

## Partner value
A serious partner may receive:
- a structured challenge definition rather than an open-ended school visit;
- fresh evidence-backed perspectives from learner teams;
- employee expert participation in a controlled format;
- prototypes or proposed interventions suitable for review;
- a governed pilot/adoption pathway;
- outcome reporting where a measurable post-adoption signal exists;
- community/education engagement evidence without access to private child data.

## School/learner value
- real-world relevance;
- access to authentic constraints and experts;
- opportunity to test ideas against reality;
- evidence/reflection for the Experience Passport;
- visible contribution without requiring public self-promotion.

## PANSOFIE value
- stronger Experience quality;
- repeatable partner-funded delivery route;
- real adoption/outcome evidence;
- a growing knowledge base of problems, solutions and lessons.

## What a partner does NOT buy
- guaranteed adoption;
- guaranteed positive impact report;
- influence over learner evaluation;
- access to raw private reflection/evidence by default;
- publicity rights to children;
- ownership of learner work by default;
- product placement or compulsory brand advocacy.

## Core design rule
`COMMERCIAL VALUE` must be downstream of `SAFE EDUCATIONAL VALUE`, never the reverse.
