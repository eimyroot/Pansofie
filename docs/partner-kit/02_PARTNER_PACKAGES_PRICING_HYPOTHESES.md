# PANSOFIE × FIRMY — PARTNER PACKAGES & PRICING HYPOTHESES V1.0

Status: COMMERCIAL HYPOTHESES — NOT A FINAL PRICE LIST

Prices below are test bands for discovery conversations. They are not validated market prices and exclude VAT/pass-through costs where applicable.

## Package A — PILOT CHALLENGE
Includes challenge intake/screening, one bounded Challenge design, school/teacher coordination, one partner briefing/review session, adoption decision capture and concise evidence/outcome report.

**Hypothesis band:** CZK 35,000–60,000 per bounded challenge.

Primary test: will a partner pay for a real governed education/innovation process rather than sponsorship visibility?

## Package B — PROGRAM PARTNER
For 2–3 Challenges or a semester portfolio. Adds repeated challenge design, employee expert onboarding, portfolio reporting, partner learning review and repeat/adoption tracking.

**Hypothesis band:** CZK 100,000–200,000 per semester/portfolio.

## Package C — STRATEGIC / TERRITORIAL PARTNER
For a municipality, larger employer, foundation or multi-school portfolio. Adds multiple Challenges/schools, governance cadence, knowledge synthesis, agreed outcome framework and custom reporting/integration.

**Hypothesis starting band:** CZK 250,000+ per annual programme. Quote only after scope discovery.

## Alternative funding modes
- foundation/grant-funded access;
- municipality-funded local Challenge portfolio;
- company funding school participation without branding pressure;
- co-funded partner/school programme;
- in-kind capability contribution valued separately from cash.

## Pricing experiment rules
Record buyer role, budget owner, accepted/rejected band, objection, substitute, procurement friction and whether a paid next-step conversation is accepted.

Do not lower price merely to obtain a positive survey. Validate willingness to pay against delivery cost and repeat value.
